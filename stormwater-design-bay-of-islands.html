Bay of Islands Stormwater Engineers

The Bay of Islands presents some of the most complex stormwater challenges in Northland. Its combination of steep hillsides, sensitive coastal and estuarine environments, volcanic and sedimentary soils, and fast-growing development pressure requires stormwater engineers who truly understand the local context. Gumboots Consulting Engineers has worked throughout the Bay of Islands since 2018, designing stormwater systems for every development type from remote lifestyle blocks to waterfront commercial projects.

Stormwater management across the Bay of Islands must satisfy both FNDC's building consent requirements and Northland Regional Council's resource consent conditions, which can include strict limits on discharge to coastal water bodies. Our team is experienced in navigating both councils' requirements, providing stormwater designs that achieve consent efficiently.

We serve all Bay of Islands communities including Paihia, Russell, Kerikeri, Opua, Waitangi, Haruru, Kaikohe, and surrounding areas.

Stormwater Services Across the Bay of Islands

Stormwater Management Plans

Comprehensive stormwater management plans for Bay of Islands developments, addressing FNDC and NRC requirements. We account for coastal sensitivity, slope, and soil conditions specific to each site.

  • Site-specific catchment analysis
  • Impervious surface calculations
  • FNDC and NRC compliance
  • Consent application support

Coastal Drainage Solutions

Stormwater design specifically for coastal Bay of Islands properties, managing runoff to protect sensitive marine and estuarine environments. We design systems that meet NRC coastal discharge limits.

  • Coastal stormwater quality treatment
  • Sediment and contaminant removal
  • NRC discharge consent support
  • Marine environment protection

Detention & Attenuation Design

Stormwater detention tanks, ponds, and attenuation systems sized to manage peak flows from Bay of Islands developments, preventing downstream flooding and satisfying discharge consent conditions.

  • Underground detention tanks
  • Detention pond design
  • Flow attenuation calculations
  • Outlet and overflow design

Subdivision Stormwater Design

Comprehensive stormwater engineering for Bay of Islands subdivisions, including reticulation design, reserve design, and consent support for multi-lot developments across the region.

  • Reticulation pipe design
  • Stormwater reserve design
  • Multi-lot catchment analysis
  • S223/S224c engineering approval
