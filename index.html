Who We Are

Geotechnical Experts
Northland & Coromandel

Established in 2018, Gumboots Consulting Engineers provides specialised geotechnical and civil engineering services throughout Northland, with a focus on Kerikeri, the Far North District, and Kaipara.

We deliver expert site assessments, geotechnical investigations, and engineering solutions tailored to the unique soil and environmental conditions of each region we serve. Our primary focus is residential developments and land assessment, providing sound professional engineering judgment with high regard for technical practicality.

With offices in Kerikeri and Whangamata, we bring deep local knowledge to projects throughout Northland — from Whangarei to the Far North — and across the Coromandel Peninsula, from Thames to Coromandel Town.

Need planning approval for your Far North project? We work alongside Far North Resource Consent consultants who handle Far North District Council resource consent applications for Kerikeri, Paihia, Kaitaia and Doubtless Bay properties.
