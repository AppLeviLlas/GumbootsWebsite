Engineering a Whangarei Subdivision Needs

Whangarei District Council assesses subdivision engineering against its Environmental Engineering Standards. We prepare each piece in-house, so the reports agree with each other.

1

Geotechnical Assessment for Each Lot

Bearing capacity, slope stability and a building platform for every proposed lot.

2

Stormwater Design

Overland flow paths, drainage and discharge, designed to WDC and Northland Regional Council requirements.

3

Wastewater

TP58 onsite wastewater design for lots without town sewer.

4

Roading, Access and Earthworks

Access roads, vehicle crossings, service corridors and earthworks design.

5

Erosion and Sediment Control

Erosion and sediment control plans for the earthworks stage, to Northland Regional Council requirements.

From Feasibility to 224(c)

1

Feasibility

Before you pay for survey and planning, we check whether the land can be subdivided from an engineering point of view: lot suitability, wastewater options and infrastructure.

2

Consent Reports

The engineering reports that go with your planner's subdivision consent application. A complete, consistent set helps avoid council requests for more information.

3

Engineering Plan Approval

Detailed design of roads, drainage and services for WDC engineering approval.

4

Construction and 224(c)

Monitoring during the works and the completion documentation council needs before it issues the section 224(c) certificate.

What It Costs

Full subdivision engineering packages start from $8,000+ for a simple two-lot subdivision. The final cost depends on the number of lots, the ground and whether the site has town sewer.
