## Listing Optimization Tips

### For Each Directory:

1. **Complete Profile 100%**
   - Fill every available field
   - Add business description (customize for each platform)
   - Include all services
   - Add business hours
   - Upload logo and photos

2. **Use Keywords Naturally**
   - Geotechnical engineer Kerikeri
   - TP58 wastewater design
   - Civil engineering Far North
   - Stormwater design Bay of Islands

3. **Add Photos**
   - Logo
   - Team photos
   - Project photos
   - Office/vehicle photos
   - Minimum 5-10 photos per listing

4. **Include Service Areas**
   - List all locations you serve
   - Kerikeri, Paihia, Bay of Islands, etc.

5. **Add Categories**
   - Primary: Engineering Consultant
   - Secondary: Geotechnical Engineer, Civil Engineer, Structural Engineer

6. **Link to Website**
   - Always include your website URL
   - Use tracking parameters if possible (e.g., ?utm_source=finda)

7. **Encourage Reviews**
   - On platforms that allow reviews
   - Respond to all reviews professionally

## Red Flags to Avoid

❌ **Don't:**
- Use different business names across platforms
- Create duplicate listings
- Use PO Box if you have physical address
- Ignore negative reviews
- Stuff keywords unnaturally
- Use fake reviews or incentivize reviews
- Let listings become outdated
- Forget to claim existing listings

✅ **Do:**
- Keep NAP consistent everywhere
- Respond to all reviews (positive and negative)
- Update listings when information changes
- Use real photos from your business
- Encourage genuine customer reviews
- Monitor listings regularly
- Build citations gradually and naturally
