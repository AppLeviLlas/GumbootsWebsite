Northland Wastewater Specialists

Gumboots Consulting Engineers offers expert wastewater design services throughout Northland. We understand the diverse requirements of the region, from the specific regulations of Far North District Council, Kaipara District Council, and Whangarei District Council to the environmental protection standards of the Northland Regional Council.

Whether you are building a new home in rural Kaitaia, upgrading a farm system in Kaikohe, developing a coastal subdivision in Dargaville, or planning a property in Whangarei, our team has the local knowledge to design a system that works. We evaluate soil types, site constraints, and usage patterns to deliver the most effective solution.

Our comprehensive service includes site assessments, soil testing, system design, and consent documentation. We are committed to providing Northland property owners with robust, low-maintenance wastewater systems that protect public health and our beautiful environment.
