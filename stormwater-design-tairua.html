Tairua & Pauanui Stormwater Design Specialists

Stormwater design in the Tairua area reflects the town's position at the foot of the Coromandel Ranges, where steep hillside catchments drain rapidly to the low-lying estuarine margins of Tairua Harbour. Tairua's residential areas sit on relatively flat ground between the hills and the harbour, with estuarine soils that have low permeability — soakpits in these soils typically drain slowly and may require careful sizing to avoid overflow during heavy rain events. The harbour estuary itself is subject to WRC's rules under the Waikato Regional Plan, and stormwater discharge to the estuary or any drain connecting to it requires careful design to meet water quality requirements.

Pauanui, on the opposite side of the harbour on the coastal barrier beach, has permeable dune sands that are much better suited to soakage-based stormwater disposal. Soakpits in Pauanui's sands drain quickly, but the shallow water table beneath the barrier limits soakpit depth. The flat topography of the Pauanui barrier also means drainage gradients are low, and site grading design is important to ensure surface runoff does not pond between buildings. WRC's coastal marine area setback rules apply on both the ocean and harbour sides of the Pauanui barrier.

We prepare stormwater management plans for TCDC building consent for both Tairua and Pauanui, designing disposal systems appropriate to the specific soil conditions on each side of the harbour.

Stormwater Management Plans — Tairua

Full stormwater management plans for TCDC building consent applications in Tairua and Pauanui.   • TCDC consent documentation
  • Rainfall volume calculations
  • Site-specific disposal design
  • Harbour estuary compliance

Soakpit Design — Tairua & Pauanui

Soakpit and soakage trench design for both sides of the harbour. In Tairua's estuarine soils, soakpits must be carefully sized for the lower infiltration rates. In Pauanui's dune sands, soakpits drain quickly but require a groundwater separation check. We design appropriately for each soil type and provide calculations to TCDC's requirements.

  • Soakpit sizing calculations
  • Soil infiltration assessment
  • Groundwater separation check
  • Overflow design

Retention & Detention — Tairua

Retention and detention design for Tairua properties where soakage capacity is limited by estuarine soils or where TCDC requires flow attenuation before discharge. For sites adjacent to the Tairua Harbour estuary, we design retention systems that capture first-flush runoff and limit peak discharge rates to meet WRC's stormwater quality requirements.

  • Retention tank sizing
  • Flow attenuation design
  • First-flush capture
  • Estuary discharge compliance

Site Drainage Design — Tairua & Pauanui

Surface and subsoil drainage design for Tairua and Pauanui residential properties. On the flat barrier beach at Pauanui, good site grading is essential to prevent ponding between buildings. In Tairua, subsoil drainage helps manage the shallow groundwater that can affect low-lying sections near the harbour. We design drainage that works with the terrain and soil conditions at each site.

  • Site grading design
  • Subsoil drainage design
  • Surface drainage channels
  • Coastal setback compliance
