Northland's Stormwater Engineering Specialists

Northland's diverse landscapes — from the volcanic soils of the Bay of Islands to the peat flatlands of the Kaipara — each present unique stormwater challenges. Gumboots Consulting Engineers has the regional knowledge and engineering expertise to design effective, compliant stormwater solutions wherever your project is located in Northland.

We work with both Far North District Council and Northland Regional Council, understanding the specific stormwater requirements that apply to different catchments and environments across the region. Our designs are prepared to satisfy both building consent and resource consent requirements from the outset, minimising delays and re-submission rounds.

Our Northland stormwater engineering services cover: Kerikeri, Paihia, Bay of Islands, Mangonui, Coopers Beach, Doubtless Bay, Kaitaia, Dargaville, Kaikohe, and all surrounding rural areas.

Stormwater Services Across Northland

Stormwater Management Plans – Northland

Stormwater management plans prepared for FNDC and NRC consent applications across all of Northland. We customise each plan to the specific location, soil type, and council requirements.

  • Catchment assessment and analysis
  • FNDC and NRC compliance
  • Residential and rural design
  • Consent application ready

Detention System Design – Northland

Design of stormwater detention and retention systems for Northland developments. Sized to manage peak flows from varied Northland terrain including hillside, flat, and coastal sites.

  • Tank and pond sizing
  • Flow attenuation design
  • Outlet and overflow design
  • Pre- and post-development comparison

Low-Impact Design – Northland

Sustainable stormwater solutions suited to Northland's natural environments. Rain gardens, swales, and bioretention systems that treat stormwater and integrate with Northland's ecology.

  • Bioretention and rain garden design
  • Grassed swale design
  • Water quality improvement
  • NRC coastal discharge compliance

Rural & Lifestyle Block Stormwater

Practical stormwater solutions for Northland's rural lifestyle blocks and farm developments. We design simple, robust systems appropriate for rural properties across the Far North.

  • Rural property drainage design
  • Cut-off drain systems
  • Farm track and access drainage
  • Lifestyle block consent support
