Engineering for Coromandel Town & the Northern Peninsula

Coromandel Town is the historic heart of the northern Coromandel Peninsula, sitting on the western harbour edge at the base of the rugged Coromandel Range. The town and surrounding district have a strong heritage of gold mining, and that history has left a legacy of mine workings, shaft locations, and mineralised ground that can affect development sites in the area. Any significant earthworks or new development in the northern peninsula should include a desktop review of historic mine workings as part of the geotechnical assessment.

Beyond the mining heritage, the northern Coromandel's terrain is some of the most rugged on the peninsula — steep volcanic ranges, narrow coastal terraces, and limited flat development land. Lifestyle blocks are common and typically require off-grid wastewater systems, careful stormwater management on steep terrain, and geotechnical assessment of highly weathered volcanic soils. Our team provides all the engineering support Coromandel Town and northern peninsula properties need for TCDC building and resource consent applications.
