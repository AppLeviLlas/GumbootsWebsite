Waihi Beach Civil Engineering Specialists

Waihi Beach is a long, exposed surf beach backed by Holocene coastal dune sands — a well-established residential and holiday destination on the southern Coromandel coast. Civil engineering here involves two quite different environments depending on where your section sits: the ocean-facing coastal dune strip, and the Waihi Estuary (Te Puna o Wharekaho) margin on the inland side of the township, where soft estuarine silts and organic clays occur at shallow depth.

On the dune-side properties, civil engineering challenges include achieving positive drainage on flat sandy lots with shallow groundwater, managing stormwater without concentrating runoff toward the coastal erosion zone, and designing infrastructure within TCDC's coastal hazard setback requirements. On estuary-margin properties, the soft compressible soils introduce additional considerations for drainage system design, subsoil drainage, and earthworks that must be addressed in the civil plans.

Waihi Beach also sits close to the historic Waihi goldfields town, and WRC's coastal marine area jurisdiction applies along the beach and estuary frontages. Our team prepares coordinated civil engineering packages that address all of these factors in a single consent-ready submission, avoiding the delays that come from piecemeal engineering submissions to TCDC.

Site Grading & Drainage Design — Waihi Beach

Earthworks and drainage designs tailored to Waihi Beach's two main site environments — flat coastal dune lots on the ocean side and softer estuary-margin ground toward the back of the township. We design drainage gradients and earthworks strategies appropriate to each environment, with coastal setback compliance built into the design from the outset.

  • Estuary margin earthworks design

  • TCDC consent-ready drawings

Stormwater Management Design — Waihi Beach

Stormwater management plans for Waihi Beach residential and holiday development, designed to manage runoff on sandy coastal lots without creating drainage conflicts near the beach or estuary margins. WRC coastal marine area rules apply along the beachfront and estuary, and we prepare stormwater designs that satisfy both TCDC and WRC requirements in one integrated submission.

  • Stormwater management plans
  • Coastal and estuary margin drainage
  • TCDC and WRC compliance
  • Drainage calculations
