Common Erosion Problems We Solve in Northland

Northland's high rainfall, clay-rich soils, and hilly terrain make erosion one of the most common engineering problems in the region. If you're seeing any of the following, you need an engineer's assessment before it gets worse.

Stream bank erosion

Banks cutting back, undermining fences or tracks. Common throughout the Far North after heavy rain events.

Slope erosion & slipping

Bare or steep slopes losing topsoil, showing rills, or beginning to slip. Requires a geotechnical assessment to determine the cause and right solution.

Driveway & access track erosion

Rural driveways and tracks washing out after rain. Drainage and erosion control can usually solve this without full reconstruction.

Construction site erosion & sediment

Resource consent for earthworks requires an erosion and sediment control plan (ESCP). We prepare these for building and subdivision consents throughout Northland.

Cut slopes near houses

Earthworks cuts that are now bare and eroding — especially common on lifestyle blocks where the original earthworks weren't properly stabilised.

Coastal & estuarine erosion

Coastal bank retreat and estuarine erosion is a growing issue in Northland. We assess risk and recommend appropriate protection or managed retreat options.

Erosion Control Services — Northland

Erosion & Sediment Control Plans (ESCP)

Required by FNDC, NRC, and WDC for any earthworks consent. We prepare practical, council-accepted ESCPs for residential earthworks, subdivisions, and roading projects throughout Northland.

  • Building and subdivision consent
  • NRC earthworks consent
  • Sediment pond & silt fence design
  • Stabilisation sequencing

Slope Stabilisation

Engineering solutions for unstable or eroding slopes — from bioengineering and vegetation to engineered earthworks, retaining structures, and drainage. Our geotechnical assessment determines the right solution for your site.

  • Slope stability analysis
  • Bioengineering recommendations
  • Drainage solutions for wet slopes
  • Retaining wall specifications

Stream Bank & Riparian Protection

Engineering assessment and design for eroding stream banks, including riprap, live willow staking, and bioengineering approaches suited to Northland's waterways and NRC requirements.

  • Stream bank erosion assessment
  • Riparian planting plans
  • Rock revetment design
  • NRC consent support

Stormwater & Drainage for Erosion Control

Most erosion is driven by water — improper drainage, concentrated overland flow, or uncontrolled runoff. We combine stormwater design with erosion control to address the root cause, not just the symptom.

  • Overland flow path management
  • Diversion drain design
  • Culvert sizing & design
  • Outlet protection
