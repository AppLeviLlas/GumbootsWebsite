When Do You Need an Engineered Retaining Wall?

In New Zealand, retaining walls over 1.5m high (or any height if surcharges like vehicles or buildings apply) require building consent and an engineer's design. Northland's hilly terrain means retaining walls are one of our most common projects.

Creating a building platform

Cutting into a slope to create a flat area for a house or garage nearly always requires engineered retaining. We design the wall and provide the geotechnical report for consent.

Driveway cuts & access

Steep driveways in Paihia, Bay of Islands, and Kerikeri often require retaining on the cut side. Vehicle surcharge increases the engineering requirement.

Replacing a failing wall

Old timber or block walls that are leaning, cracking, or failing need a proper assessment before repair or replacement — to avoid rebuilding the same problem.

Subdivision earthworks

Retaining walls are common in subdivision development to maximise useable land area on sloping sites. We design these as part of the full subdivision engineering package.

Retaining Wall Engineering Services

Retaining Wall Structural Design

Full structural engineering design for timber, concrete block, concrete, and steel retaining walls. We produce consent-ready drawings and calculations accepted by FNDC and WDC.

  • Timber pole & plank walls
  • Concrete block (segmental) walls
  • Poured concrete walls
  • Steel sheet pile walls

Geotechnical Input for Retaining Walls

Every retaining wall design needs geotechnical parameters — soil bearing capacity, lateral earth pressures, and backfill specifications. Our geotechnical reports provide this as part of the design process.

  • Soil investigation & testing
  • Lateral earth pressure assessment
  • Backfill specifications
  • Foundation bearing capacity

Retaining Wall Drainage Design

Drainage failure is the leading cause of retaining wall collapse. We design sub-surface drainage systems behind retaining walls to manage water and extend wall life.

  • Subsoil drain design
  • Free-draining backfill specification
  • Weep hole placement
  • Surface water management

Building Consent Documentation

We prepare the full consent package for your retaining wall — engineering drawings, calculations, specifications, and producer statement — to streamline your FNDC or WDC building consent.

  • PS1 design producer statement
  • Consent-ready drawings & calcs
  • PS3 construction monitoring (if required)
  • PS4 sign-off on completion
