Whangarei Stormwater Design Specialists

Whangarei's ongoing growth — from urban infill in the city to new residential development along the coast at Ruakaka and Waipu — means stormwater management is increasingly critical for getting building consents and resource consents approved by Whangarei District Council. Our engineers design practical, compliant stormwater systems that meet WDC's design standards without overcomplicating your project.

The Whangarei District presents diverse stormwater challenges. Hillside residential developments must manage steep catchments and erosion risk, while properties near Whangarei Harbour face sensitivity to sediment and contaminant discharge. Coastal areas at Ruakaka and Waipu require careful drainage design that protects nearby dune systems and estuarine environments. Our team has experience designing solutions across all of these settings.

Whether you need a stormwater management plan for a new dwelling, a comprehensive drainage design for a subdivision, or erosion and sediment control for a construction site, Gumboots Consulting Engineers delivers professional stormwater engineering backed by genuine Whangarei knowledge.

Stormwater Services in Whangarei

Compliant stormwater engineering solutions for Whangarei's diverse development types

Stormwater Management Plans

Comprehensive stormwater management plans prepared to Whangarei District Council standards. We calculate impervious areas, design detention volumes, and document management measures for your WDC consent application.

  • Catchment area analysis
  • Impervious surface calculations
  • WDC-compliant reporting
  • Consent application support

Drainage System Design

Design of stormwater drainage systems for residential, commercial, and subdivision sites in Whangarei. We size pipes, channels, and detention systems to manage peak flows and meet WDC discharge requirements.

  • Pipe network design
  • Channel and swale design
  • Outlet and discharge design
  • Volume and flow calculations

Erosion & Sediment Control

Erosion and sediment control plans for construction sites in Whangarei. We design practical control measures to protect Whangarei Harbour and receiving environments during earthworks and construction activities.

  • ESC plan preparation
  • Silt fence and sediment trap design
  • Staged earthworks advice
  • NRC and WDC compliance

Detention & Retention Design

Design of stormwater detention tanks, ponds, and retention systems sized to attenuate peak flows from your Whangarei site to pre-development levels, meeting WDC's stormwater discharge requirements.

  • Underground tank sizing
  • Detention pond design
  • Overflow and outlet design
  • Climate change allowances
