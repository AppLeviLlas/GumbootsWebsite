Kerikeri's Structural Engineering Specialists

Gumboots Consulting Engineers has been Kerikeri's trusted structural engineering partner since 2018. Our CPEng-qualified engineers bring deep local knowledge to every foundation design, retaining wall, and slope stability project across the Kerikeri area.

Kerikeri's volcanic soils and clay-rich geology present unique structural engineering challenges. The basalt and pumice-derived soils common throughout the township require specific foundation approaches — from raft slabs on shrink-swell clays to piled foundations where ground conditions demand deeper bearing. Our engineers have assessed hundreds of Kerikeri sites and understand precisely how to engineer for these local conditions.

The hilly topography surrounding Kerikeri also means retaining walls are a constant feature of residential and commercial development. Whether you're terracing a lifestyle block above Kerikeri township, managing slopes near the Stone Store, or developing a sloped section in Waipapa, our structural engineers design compliant, cost-effective retaining solutions. We prepare full consent-ready documentation including producer statements and PS1 through PS4 for Far North District Council submissions.
