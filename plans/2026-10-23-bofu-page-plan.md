### 1. Site feasibility Northland

- **Buyer:** someone with a section under contract and a due-diligence clause running out
- **Compact targets:** site feasibility Northland, land assessment Whangarei, feasibility study Paihia, section report Kerikeri, pre-purchase geotech Northland
- **GSC evidence:** land assessment whangarei 93 (pos 17); property feasibility study paihia 35 (pos 2.4); site feasibility study northland 35 (pos 11.8); land assessment northland 34; development feasibility consultant northland 28 (pos 9.5); 9 more variants
- **Competitors:** LDE and Haigh Workman bury feasibility inside geotech and civil pages. No local firm has a dedicated page.
- **Sections:** what it checks (stability, soils, wastewater, stormwater, access, zoning) · desktop vs walkover vs testing · turnaround vs due-diligence clause · price (needs Paul's figure) · enquiry form asking settlement date

### 2. Slope stability & landslip Northland

- **Buyer:** owner with an active slip, or a council instability flag on a consent
- **Compact targets:** slope stability Northland, landslip engineer Whangarei, slip repair Far North, land stability report Kerikeri, slope stabilisation Hokianga
- **GSC evidence:** thin (slope stabilisation hokianga 38, pos 39). Low numbers reflect the missing page, not low demand.
- **Market evidence:** LDE assessed 380 landslides for FNDC; LDE and Haigh Workman both lead with slip work. NZGS case studies describe landslips across older and newer Northland development.
- **Site gap:** "slope stability" appears on 37 Gumboots pages but none is a landing page for it
- **Sections:** signs of instability · urgent vs planned assessment · stability report for consent · remediation options (drainage, walls, piles) · insurance and Natural Hazards Commission (ex-EQC) claims · local geology (Kerikeri volcanics, Northland Allochthon clays)

### 3. Subdivision consultants Whangarei

- **Buyer:** landowner ready to subdivide under WDC rules
- **Compact targets:** subdivision consultants Whangarei, land development Whangarei, subdivision engineer Whangarei, WDC engineering standards
- **GSC evidence:** subdivision consultants whangarei 222 (pos 18); land development services whangarei 173 (pos 15); development advisory whangarei 68; property development consultant whangarei 67; land development whangarei 50
- **Competition:** heaviest of the five (Cato Bolam, Hawthorn Geddes, LDE, Haigh Workman, all with Whangarei offices)
- **Prerequisite:** 301 `subdivision-consultant-northland.html` to `subdivision-engineering-northland.html` first (from Sep plan)
- **Differentiator:** WDC Environmental Engineering Standards, WDC process and timeline (the Northland page covers FNDC)

### 5. Foundation design & expansive soils (optional)
