# BOFU Compact-Keyword Plan — Gumboots

**Date:** 2026-11-02 (revised: consents handed off)
**Data:** GSC export, last 3 months to 31 Oct 2026. 329 clicks, 21,448 impressions, 87% NZ. 606 listed queries, 120 pages.
**Method:** Edward Sturm's compact keywords. Specific, buyer-intent, low-competition searches; one page per intent; titles that mirror the search.
**Supersedes:** `2026-10-23-bofu-page-plan.md`.

## Scope

- **Gumboots owns engineering:** geotech, soil testing, erosion & sediment control, retaining walls, stormwater, wastewater/TP58/septic, structural, subdivision engineering
- **Far North Resource Consents owns consents and planning:** resource consents, planning, development feasibility, subdivision consent. Gumboots sends that intent and link equity to resourceconsent.co.nz and doesn't compete for it.
- **Partner covers the Far North District only.** Copy on Whangarei, Kaipara and Coromandel pages must not say the partner handles consents there.

## Limits

- Live GSC unavailable (`access_denied`, OAuth expired). Query→page pairs are inferred from totals. Confirm after re-auth.
- No keyword-volume or difficulty tool. Impressions stand in for demand.
- Positions blend devices and countries. Treat them as direction, not rank.

### 2.2 Contextual links to resourceconsent.co.nz

One link per page, where consent is the natural next step after the engineering work. Vary the anchor text, and use the brand where it reads naturally.

| Gumboots page | Link to | Example anchor |
|---|---|---|
| `subdivision-engineering-northland` | `/subdivision-consent.html` | "subdivision consent in the Far North" |
| `erosion-control-northland` (ESCP) | `/resource-consent.html` | "earthworks consents" |
| `geotechnical-report` | `/do-i-need-resource-consent.html` | "whether your project needs resource consent" |
| `stormwater-design` | `/resource-consent.html` | "Far North Resource Consents" |
| New pre-purchase page (3.2) | `/development-feasibility-study.html` | "development feasibility study" |
| `index` (already links) | `/` | keep as is |

- **Avoid:** a sitewide footer link with keyword anchors. Google treats links between sites you own as fine when they help the reader. Heavy, repetitive cross-linking reads as a link scheme.
- **Keep:** follow links (no `nofollow`), since passing equity is the point
- **Keep:** neutral consent mentions in engineering copy (e.g. "reports accepted by FNDC for consent"). Remove consent terms from titles, H1s and meta descriptions only.

### Compact page template

- **Title and H1:** mirror the exact search, ≤60 chars, brand last
- **First screen:** answer the buyer's question (what you do, price range, turnaround, which council accepts it) plus one call/quote button
- **Body:** council-specific steps, local proof (a real project photo and town), short FAQ from real queries
- **Links:** from the town hub and service hub; to the next step (quote/contact); one contextual link to resourceconsent.co.nz where consent is the next step
- **Iterate:** after 4–6 weeks, read the page's queries in GSC and retitle toward what it actually shows for
