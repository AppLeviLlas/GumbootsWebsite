## What Are Compact Keywords?

**Compact keywords** are high-density, information-rich terms that:
- ✅ Maximize semantic meaning per word
- ✅ Trigger multiple related concepts
- ✅ Improve search intent matching
- ✅ Enhance cognitive efficiency for users
- ✅ Balance specificity with search volume

**Example:**
- ❌ Verbose: "geotechnical engineering services in Kerikeri"
- ✅ Compact: "geotechnical engineer Kerikeri" or "geotech Kerikeri"

## Compact Keyword Formula

### For Title Tags:
```
[Compact Keyword 1] [Location] | [Compact Keyword 2] | [Brand]
```

**Examples:**
- TP58 Kerikeri | TP58 Wastewater Design | Gumboots
- Geotechnical Engineer Kerikeri | Geotech Northland | Gumboots
- Drainage Design Kerikeri | Stormwater Solutions | Gumboots

### For Meta Descriptions:
```
[Compact Keyword] [Location], [Related Service] & [Related Service]. [Benefit]. Call [Phone].
```

**Examples:**
- TP58 wastewater design Kerikeri, Paihia & Northland. Expert TP58 engineer for septic systems. Call 020 4 GUMMYS.
- Geotechnical engineer Kerikeri - soil testing, site assessments & foundation design. Local geotech specialist. Call 020 4 GUMMYS.

### For H1 Tags:
```
[Compact Keyword] [Location] - [Benefit/Service Description]
```

**Examples:**
- TP58 Wastewater Design Kerikeri - Expert TP58 Engineer Northland
- Geotech Kerikeri - Local Geotechnical Engineering Specialist
- Drainage Design Kerikeri - Stormwater & Surface Water Solutions

## Semantic Density Scoring

### How to Score Your Keywords:

**Formula:** (Intent Clarity × Technical Specificity × Search Volume) / Word Count

**Scale:**
- **9-10:** Exceptional (e.g., "TP58", "geotech")
- **7-8:** Strong (e.g., "geotechnical Kerikeri")
- **5-6:** Moderate (e.g., "site assessment services")
- **1-4:** Low (e.g., "geotechnical engineering services in the Kerikeri area")

### Examples:

| Keyword | Word Count | Score | Why |
|---------|------------|-------|-----|
| TP58 | 1 | 10/10 | Industry-specific, high intent, technical |
| geotech Kerikeri | 2 | 9/10 | Colloquial + location, high intent |
| geotechnical engineer Kerikeri | 3 | 8/10 | Specific role + location |
| geotechnical engineering services | 3 | 6/10 | "Services" is redundant |
| geotechnical engineering services in Kerikeri | 5 | 4/10 | Too verbose, low density |

## Internal Linking Best Practices

### DO ✅
- Use compact keywords as anchor text
- Link from homepage to all key pages
- Create hub-and-spoke structure
- Use contextual links within content

**Examples:**
- "Our [TP58 wastewater design](#) services..."
- "Need a [geotech Kerikeri](#)?"
- "Expert [geotechnical engineer Kerikeri](#)"

### DON'T ❌
- Use generic anchor text ("click here", "learn more")
- Over-optimize with exact match everywhere
- Link to irrelevant pages
- Use verbose anchor text

**Bad Examples:**
- "Click here to learn more about our services"
- "We offer geotechnical engineering services in Kerikeri"
- "Read more about what we do"

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Mistake 1: Brand-First Titles
**Bad:** "Gumboots Consulting Engineers | Geotechnical Services"
**Good:** "Geotechnical Engineer Kerikeri | Geotech Northland | Gumboots"

### ❌ Mistake 2: Redundant Words
**Bad:** "geotechnical engineering services"
**Good:** "geotechnical engineering" or "geotech services"

### ❌ Mistake 3: Too Generic
**Bad:** "Engineering services in Northland"
**Good:** "TP58 engineer Northland" or "geotech Northland"

### ❌ Mistake 4: Missing Location
**Bad:** "TP58 wastewater design services"
**Good:** "TP58 wastewater design Kerikeri"

### ❌ Mistake 5: Keyword Stuffing
**Bad:** "TP58 TP58 Kerikeri TP58 wastewater TP58 design TP58 engineer"
**Good:** "TP58 wastewater design Kerikeri - expert TP58 engineer"

## Emergency Troubleshooting

### Problem: CTR Not Improving
**Solutions:**
1. A/B test different title variations
2. Add power words (expert, specialist, local)
3. Include pricing/cost in meta description
4. Add year (2025, 2026) for freshness
5. Analyze competitor snippets

### Problem: Not Ranking for New Keywords
**Solutions:**
1. Check if page is indexed (site:yoururl.com)
2. Build more internal links to the page
3. Add more content (aim for 1000+ words)
4. Ensure compact keyword is in first 100 words
5. Submit to GSC for indexing

### Problem: Rankings Dropped
**Solutions:**
1. Don't panic - fluctuations are normal
2. Check for technical issues (404s, slow load)
3. Ensure content wasn't accidentally removed
4. Check if competitors improved their content
5. Give it 2-3 weeks before making changes

### Problem: High Impressions, Low Clicks
**Solutions:**
1. Improve title tag appeal
2. Add compelling meta description
3. Include pricing/cost information
4. Add trust signals (years in business, certifications)
5. Use schema markup for rich snippets
