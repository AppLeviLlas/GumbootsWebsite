## 💡 What Are Compact Keywords?

Compact keywords are **high-density, information-rich terms** that maximize meaning per word:

### Examples:

| ❌ Verbose (Low Density) | ✅ Compact (High Density) | Improvement |
|-------------------------|-------------------------|-------------|
| geotechnical engineering services in Kerikeri | geotechnical engineer Kerikeri | 60% shorter, clearer intent |
| TP58 wastewater design services | TP58 Kerikeri | 67% shorter, location-specific |
| ground testing services nz | soil testing Kerikeri | More specific, local focus |

**Why This Matters:**
- ✅ Better search intent matching
- ✅ Higher click-through rates
- ✅ Improved user cognitive efficiency
- ✅ Stronger local SEO signals
- ✅ More competitive in SERPs

## 🤔 Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Why do I have 0 clicks despite 273 impressions?
**A:** Your titles and meta descriptions don't emphasize your strongest keywords (TP58, geotechnical) early enough. Users see competitors' more compelling snippets and click those instead.

### Q: What's the difference between "geotechnical" and "geotech"?
**A:** "Geotech" is the colloquial shorthand that many people use when searching. By targeting both, you capture different search behaviors.

### Q: How long until I see results?
**A:** 
- **Quick wins (title updates):** 1-2 weeks
- **New pages:** 2-4 weeks to start ranking
- **Significant traffic:** 60-90 days
- **Full impact:** 6-12 months

### Q: Can I do this myself or do I need help?
**A:** The quick wins (title/meta updates) are easy to do yourself. Content creation can be done in-house or outsourced. The roadmap is designed for either approach.

### Q: What if my rankings drop temporarily?
**A:** Normal during optimization. Google needs to re-evaluate your pages. Give it 2-3 weeks. Rankings typically improve after initial fluctuation.

### Q: How much time will this take?
**A:**
- **Week 1 quick wins:** 2-3 hours
- **Month 1 total:** 15-20 hours
- **Months 2-3:** 20-30 hours each
- **Ongoing:** 5-10 hours/month for maintenance
