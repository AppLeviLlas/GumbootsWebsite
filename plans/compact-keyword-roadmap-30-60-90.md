## Risk Mitigation

### Potential Challenges & Solutions

**Challenge 1: Google doesn't index new pages quickly**
- Solution: Submit to GSC immediately, build internal links, share on social media

**Challenge 2: CTR doesn't improve as expected**
- Solution: A/B test different title/meta variations, analyze competitor snippets

**Challenge 3: Rankings drop temporarily**
- Solution: Normal during optimization, monitor closely, don't panic, give it 2-3 weeks

**Challenge 4: Limited time/resources**
- Solution: Focus on quick wins first (title tags), outsource content creation if needed

**Challenge 5: Competitor response**
- Solution: Focus on unique local expertise, build better content, emphasize TP58 specialization
