### 1.3 Semantic Density Scoring Methodology

**Formula:** Semantic Density = (Intent Clarity × Technical Specificity × Search Volume Potential) / Word Count

**Scale:**
- 9-10: Exceptional compact keyword (e.g., "TP58", "geotech")
- 7-8: Strong compact keyword (e.g., "geotechnical Kerikeri")
- 5-6: Moderate efficiency (e.g., "site assessment services")
- 1-4: Low efficiency, needs optimization
