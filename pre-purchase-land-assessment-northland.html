What a Pre-Purchase Land Assessment Checks

A section can look perfect and still hide problems that add tens of thousands to a build. We look for them before you commit.

Slope Stability

Signs of past ground movement, steep or unstable slopes, and whether the building platform is safe.

Soils and Foundations

Soil type and strength, soft ground or reactive clays, and the foundation type you are likely to need.

Wastewater Suitability

If the site has no town sewer, whether the soil can take an onsite wastewater system to TP58 standards.

Stormwater and Flooding

Overland flow paths, low-lying ground and council flood hazard mapping.

Erosion and Earthworks

Erosion-prone ground and how much earthworks a building platform would need.

Council Hazard Overlays

Natural hazard and other overlays on the property that affect what council will ask for at consent.

How Deep We Go

We match the assessment to what you need to know and how long you have.

Desktop Review

Council hazard maps, geology maps and the property file, before anyone visits the site.

Site Walkover

An engineer walks the section, looks for warning signs and confirms what the maps suggest.

Testing

Hand auger holes, test pits or soakage tests where the decision depends on what is under the ground.

What You Get

  • A plain-English summary of the risks we found
  • The foundation type the site is likely to need
  • Whether onsite wastewater is feasible, and roughly what kind of system
  • Which engineering reports council will want at building consent
  • Issues that could add cost, flagged before you buy

If you go ahead, the assessment flows straight into a full geotechnical report and TP58 wastewater design for your building consent.

Timing and Cost

Pre-purchase decisions run on deadlines. Tell us your due diligence date when you get in touch and we will tell you what we can deliver by then.

For reference, a full geotechnical report for a standard Northland site costs $1,500–$2,500 + GST. A pre-purchase assessment is usually lighter. Call us for a fixed quote.
