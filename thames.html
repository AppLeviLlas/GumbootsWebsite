Engineering for Thames & the Hauraki Plains

Thames is the main service centre for the Coromandel Peninsula and the gateway to the Hauraki Plains. The town's engineering character is shaped by its position at the base of the Coromandel Range, where steep volcanic hillsides meet the former estuarine and tidal flat soils of the Hauraki Plains. These contrasting ground conditions — weathered volcanic rock on the hill margins and soft, compressible alluvial soils on the plains — create specific foundation, drainage, and stormwater engineering challenges that require careful site-specific assessment.

Thames also carries a legacy of gold and silver mining. Historic mine workings, tailings, and contaminated ground are present in parts of the township and surrounding hills. Any significant earthworks or development in historically mined areas should include a ground condition assessment to identify potential contamination or subsurface voids. Our team is experienced in identifying these risks and advising on appropriate investigation and remediation approaches for TCDC applications.
