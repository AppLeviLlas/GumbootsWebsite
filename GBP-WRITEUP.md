## Business Description (750 characters max)

Gumboots Consulting Engineers is a leading geotechnical and civil engineering firm serving Kerikeri, Paihia, Whangarei, Dargaville, Kaipara District, and throughout Northland since 2018. We specialize in site suitability assessments, geotechnical investigations, soil testing, foundation design, TP58 wastewater design, stormwater management, and structural engineering solutions.

Our team delivers expert engineering services tailored to Northland's unique soil and environmental conditions. We focus on residential developments and land assessment, providing sound professional judgment with high regard for technical practicality and safety. As proud members of Engineering New Zealand and Amotai registered suppliers, we enable positive change through valuing people with invaluable service.

## Q&A Section (Pre-populate)

**Q: What areas do you service?**
A: We service Kerikeri, Paihia, Whangarei, Bay of Islands, Dargaville, Opononi, Omapere, Hokianga, Waipapa, Opua, Russell, Kawakawa, Matauri Bay, Kaeo, Kaitaia, and throughout the Far North District, Kaipara District, and Northland region.

**Q: Do you provide TP58 wastewater design?**
A: Yes! We specialize in TP58 compliant wastewater design, onsite wastewater systems, septic system design, and land application systems for properties throughout Northland, including Far North District, Kaipara District, Whangarei, and the West Coast.

**Q: What is a site suitability assessment?**
A: A site suitability assessment evaluates your property's ground conditions, soil characteristics, and environmental factors to determine its suitability for building and development. This includes geotechnical investigations, soil testing, and foundation recommendations.

**Q: How long does a geotechnical investigation take?**
A: Timeframes vary depending on project scope, but typically a standard residential site assessment takes 1-2 weeks from initial site visit to report delivery. Contact us for specific timeframes for your project.

**Q: Are you members of Engineering New Zealand?**
A: Yes, we are proud members of Engineering New Zealand and Amotai registered suppliers, ensuring professional standards and quality service.

**Q: Do you provide resource consent support?**
A: Yes, we provide engineering support for resource consent applications, including wastewater design, stormwater management plans, and geotechnical assessments required by Far North District Council, Kaipara District Council, and Whangarei District Council.

**Q: What is your consultation process?**
A: Contact us via phone (020 4 GUMMYS) or email (office@gumbootsconsulting.co.nz) to discuss your project. We'll arrange a site visit, conduct necessary investigations, and provide a comprehensive engineering report with recommendations.

**Q: Do you work with residential and commercial projects?**
A: We work on all projects requiring geitechincal, wastewater and stormwater design, which inlcudes commercial and industrial projects throughout Northland, including Far North District, Kaipara District, and Whangarei. Contact us to discuss your specific needs.
