TP58 Wastewater Specialists on the Coromandel Peninsula

On-site wastewater design is critical across the Coromandel Peninsula, where reticulated sewerage networks are limited and most rural and coastal properties rely on septic systems. Gumboots Consulting Engineers provides TP58-compliant wastewater assessments from our Whangamata office.

The Coromandel's soils vary significantly across the Peninsula—from the free-draining coastal sands of Whangamata and Hot Water Beach to the tight volcanic clays inland and the alluvial river flats near Thames. Each site needs a site-specific wastewater assessment, and we provide exactly that.

We work to Auckland Regional Council Technical Publication 58 (TP58) standards, which are required by Thames-Coromandel District Council and Waikato Regional Council for all onsite wastewater systems. Our reports are prepared to support resource consent applications and building consents across the region.

Soil Permeability Testing

We carry out soil permeability tests across the Coromandel Peninsula to determine the suitability and size of your wastewater disposal field. Tests are completed on-site by our engineers.

  • Falling head permeability tests
  • Soil profile assessments
  • Groundwater depth checks
  • Setback distance analysis

TP58 Assessment Reports

Comprehensive TP58 wastewater assessment reports prepared to TCDC and WRC standards. Our reports are accepted by council and support both resource consent and building consent applications.

  • Full TP58 assessment reports
  • Resource consent support
  • Building consent support
