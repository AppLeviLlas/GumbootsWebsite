When Do You Need a Stormwater Management Plan?

A stormwater management plan (SMP) is a council consent document that shows how stormwater from your site will be managed — ensuring it doesn't create flooding, erosion, or contamination downstream. It is required for most new builds and developments in Northland.

New residential builds

Most building consents in FNDC and WDC require a stormwater management plan showing roof drainage, driveway runoff, and site drainage to a legal point of discharge.

Subdivision

Subdivisions require a comprehensive SMP addressing stormwater infrastructure for all lots, including overland flow paths, detention, and discharge to waterways or council systems.

Commercial & industrial development

Large impervious areas like car parks and industrial yards generate significant stormwater. SMPs for commercial sites include treatment and detention design to meet NRC discharge rules.

Sites near waterways or flood zones

Properties near streams, rivers, or areas with a history of flooding need a flood risk assessment as part of their SMP — required by NRC and most building consents in these areas.
