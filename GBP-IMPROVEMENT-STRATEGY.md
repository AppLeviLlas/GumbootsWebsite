## 🚀 Priority 2: Review Generation & Management

### 2.1 Why Reviews Matter for Rankings

Google's local ranking factors:
- **Review Quantity:** 15+ reviews significantly improves rankings
- **Review Velocity:** Regular new reviews signal active business
- **Review Recency:** Recent reviews (last 30 days) carry more weight
- **Review Ratings:** 4.5+ star average is ideal
- **Review Keywords:** Reviews mentioning services/locations help rankings
- **Review Responses:** 100% response rate signals engagement

### 2.2 Review Generation System

**Goal:** 2-4 new reviews per month

**Implementation:**

**Step 1: Create Direct Review Link**
1. Go to your GBP dashboard
2. Navigate to "Home" → "Get more reviews"
3. Copy your unique review link
4. Shorten it: `https://g.page/r/[YOUR_CODE]/review`

**Step 2: Email Template (Send 7-10 days after project completion)**

```
Subject: How was your experience with Gumboots Consulting Engineers?

Hi [Client Name],

Thank you for choosing Gumboots Consulting Engineers for your [project type] in [location]. We hope you're pleased with the results!

If you have a moment, we'd greatly appreciate your feedback on Google. Your review helps other property owners in the Far North District find quality engineering services.

Leave a review: [YOUR DIRECT REVIEW LINK]

If you have any questions or concerns about your project, please don't hesitate to contact us directly at office@gumbootsconsulting.co.nz or 020 4 GUMMYS.

Thank you for your business!

The Gumboots Team
```

**Step 3: In-Person Request Script**

At project completion meeting:
> "We're glad we could help with your project. If you were satisfied with our service, we'd really appreciate if you could share your experience on Google. It helps other property owners in [location] find us. I can send you a link that makes it easy - would that be okay?"

**Step 4: Follow-Up Touchpoints**
- Email signature: "Satisfied with our service? [Leave us a review]"
- Invoice footer: "Review us on Google" with link
- Website: "Client Reviews" page with prominent review button
- Social media: Monthly post encouraging reviews

### 2.3 Review Response Strategy

**Response Time:** Within 24 hours (critical ranking factor)

**5-Star Review Template:**

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Keyword Stuffing
❌ **Wrong:** "Geotechnical engineer Kerikeri geotechnical engineering Kerikeri best geotechnical Kerikeri"
✅ **Right:** "Expert geotechnical engineering services in Kerikeri and Bay of Islands"

### 2. Inconsistent NAP
❌ **Wrong:** Different phone numbers, addresses, or business names across platforms
✅ **Right:** Identical NAP everywhere

### 3. Ignoring Reviews
❌ **Wrong:** Not responding to reviews or only responding to negative ones
✅ **Right:** Respond to ALL reviews within 24-48 hours

### 4. Inactive Profile
❌ **Wrong:** No posts for months, old photos, outdated information
✅ **Right:** Regular posts, fresh photos, current information

### 5. Wrong Category
❌ **Wrong:** Using "Consultant" as primary category
✅ **Right:** Using "Geotechnical Engineer" or "Engineering Consultant" as primary

### 6. Fake Reviews
❌ **Wrong:** Asking employees to leave reviews, incentivizing reviews, fake reviews
✅ **Right:** Genuine review requests to real clients after service completion

### 7. Duplicate Listings
❌ **Wrong:** Multiple GBP listings for same business
✅ **Right:** One verified listing (merge duplicates)

### 8. Poor Quality Photos
❌ **Wrong:** Blurry, dark, irrelevant stock photos
✅ **Right:** High-quality, well-lit photos of actual work, team, and locations
