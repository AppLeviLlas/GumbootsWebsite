Why One Engineering Firm for Everything?

When your stormwater engineer, geotechnical engineer, and wastewater designer are all in different firms, they each write their reports in isolation. Inconsistencies between reports are the most common cause of Section 92 Requests for Further Information from FNDC — and each RFI can add weeks to your consent timeline.

At Gumboots, geotechnical, civil, stormwater, and wastewater design are all done by the same team, in the same office, with the same site knowledge. When the stormwater report references the soakage rates from the geotech report, they match — because they came from the same investigation. When the wastewater design references the site constraints, they're consistent with the civil plan. Applications lodged by a single coordinated team go through faster with fewer RFIs.

There's also a cost benefit. Travel time to site is shared across all disciplines — one site visit instead of three. Administrative overhead is halved. And when conditions need to be addressed post-consent, there's one phone call, not three.

What Makes Gumboots Different?

Most engineering firms in Northland are based in Whangarei and travel to the Far North on request. Gumboots is based in Kerikeri — we're already there. That means faster response times, lower travel costs passed on to you, and engineers who have worked on hundreds of local sites and know the ground conditions intimately.

We've built specific expertise with Far North District Council's requirements. FNDC has different standards and approaches than Whangarei or Auckland councils — their specific requirements for geotechnical reports, stormwater management plans, and TP58 wastewater designs are well understood by our team. Reports written to FNDC's standards don't come back with RFIs asking for additional information that council already has templates for.

We're also straightforward about scope. If your project doesn't need a particular report, we'll tell you upfront. Pre-application advice is always free — call us before committing to any engineering spend and we'll give you an honest assessment of what's needed.
