Septic Systems & TP58 — What's the Difference?

In Northland, "septic system design" and "TP58" are the same thing. TP58 is the Northland Regional Council's technical publication that governs how onsite wastewater systems (septic tanks, effluent disposal fields, aerobic treatment units, etc.) must be designed and installed. If you need a septic system, you need a TP58 report.

When do you need a TP58 report?
  • Building consent for a new dwelling off the council sewer
  • Adding a bedroom or dwelling unit (increases wastewater load)
  • Replacing a failing or non-compliant septic system
  • Subdivision — every lot that can't connect to sewer
  • Change of use increasing wastewater generation
Who is affected in Northland?

The majority of rural and lifestyle block properties in Northland are not connected to reticulated sewer and require an onsite wastewater system. This includes most of:

  • Far North District rural properties
  • Kerikeri lifestyle blocks & new subdivisions
  • Bay of Islands, Paihia, Mangonui, Coopers Beach
  • Kaipara District rural properties
  • Whangarei rural & semi-rural areas
