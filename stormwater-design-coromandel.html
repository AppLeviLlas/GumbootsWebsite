Coromandel Peninsula Stormwater Design Specialists

The Coromandel Peninsula receives some of New Zealand's highest rainfall totals. Managing stormwater effectively is critical for any development on the Peninsula—from a coastal bach upgrade in Whangamata to a lifestyle subdivision near Thames. Gumboots Consulting Engineers brings specialist stormwater expertise to every project.

We design stormwater systems suited to the Coromandel's varied terrain—from flat coastal sites where soakage is possible to steep hillside properties requiring careful overland flow management and erosion protection. Every design is site-specific and engineered for the Peninsula's rainfall intensities.

Our stormwater reports are prepared to meet Thames-Coromandel District Council and Waikato Regional Council requirements. We provide the design drawings, calculations, and engineering assessments that support resource consent and building consent applications across the region.

Stormwater Design Services Across the Coromandel Peninsula

Drainage and stormwater engineering for Whangamata, Thames, Tairua, Hahei, Hot Water Beach, and all Coromandel communities

Stormwater Management Plans

Comprehensive stormwater management plans for Coromandel developments. We assess site runoff, design treatment and disposal systems, and prepare council-accepted SMPs.

  • Site runoff calculations
  • Attenuation design
  • Treatment device selection
  • Council SMP documentation

Drainage Design

Site drainage design for residential and rural properties across the Coromandel. We design systems that manage roof water, surface water, and subsurface drainage effectively.

  • Roof water disposal
  • Surface water drainage
  • Cut-off drain design
  • Soakage system design

Consent Engineering Reports

Engineering assessments for stormwater-related resource consents. We prepare the engineering sections of AEE documents and work with your planner to support consent applications.

  • AEE engineering sections
  • Stormwater consent reports
  • Flood assessment
  • Overland flow analysis

Erosion & Sediment Control

Erosion and sediment control planning for earthworks and construction on the Coromandel. We prepare ESC plans that meet TCDC and WRC requirements.

  • ESC plan preparation
  • Construction phase management
  • Sediment control design
  • Post-construction restoration
